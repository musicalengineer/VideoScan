## Remaining findings

### R1 — P2: first provisional snapshot permits catalog removal through a new alias

At startup, or just after adding a scan target such as
`/Users/test/CatalogAlias -> /Volumes/FamilyArchive/MoviesExpansion`, the
provisional snapshot has not learned that alias. Its verdict is `.clear`, so
catalog removal of non-promoted archive-volume records can proceed before the
background identity build finishes.

At the reviewed identity snapshot:

- `VideoScanModel+ArchiveVolumeSnapshot.swift:76–78` omits pending scan-target
  aliases when constructing the provisional snapshot.
- `ArchiveVolumeProtection.swift:352,399` starts with empty alias roots and
  returns clear for a non-`/Volumes` spelling.
- `VideoScanModel+MasterArchive.swift:697` permits the resulting clear verdict.

Reproduced with production helper method bodies and the extracted catalog-removal
gate. Previously resolved aliases survive invalidation correctly; the first/new
alias is the missing case. Defer removal for unresolved scan-target prefixes
until their identities are established. This affects catalog metadata, not the
physical-removal path, which performs its own final identity check.

### R2 — P2: live copy selection silently ignores members beyond 64

At the reviewed S3 snapshot, `ArchiveAngelJob+Evidence.swift:227–229` takes only
64 members including the anchor before checking in-flight membership or choosing
the live Keep copy. In a group of 65+, a newly selected Keep or an already
prepared member outside that prefix is invisible. Prepare can choose the wrong
copy or prepare the recording again.

Source-confirmed; no full runtime group-selection harness was executed. Preserve
the bound by declining the cached path and falling back to the complete selection
when a group exceeds the limit, rather than treating a prefix as the full group.
