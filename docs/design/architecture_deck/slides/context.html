Context and problem statement

User story, in Rick’s words

“Make the corpus easier to search and organize. A date within 2–3 years is valuable; ‘find all the videos down the Cape from 1990–1995’ answered 90 % right is a win. Then the same for places, events, people.”

People search like Google, not like a metadata expert: “Donna down the Cape when Timmy says peekaboo, probably in the 90s.” The search bar and Hallie are the two doors; one engine behind them.

The problem

  • About 8 TB and 450 hours of family footage across many drives, with copies, transfers and re-digitizations of the same tape
  • Film and tape carry no metadata; a transfer stamps its own date over the original
  • Who is in it, when, where and what occasion lives only in memory
  • Nothing may ever be lost, and the archive must stay fixed

Goals, in order

  1. Catalog every file on every drive
  2. Curate and analyze: dates, people, events
  3. Promote the unique, dispose of duplicates
  4. Present media in meaningful ways
  5. Grow the family tree and its knowledge
  6. Hallie Mae, the family archivist
  7. Keep Rick engaged in joyful work
