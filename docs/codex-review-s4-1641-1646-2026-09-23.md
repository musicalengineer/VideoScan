## 1. P1 — sampled signatures still authorize known-fact inheritance

`ArchiveAngel/Review/ArchiveAngelShowCopies.swift:68,124` indexes and walks equal
`contentHash` values in `collectIdentity`. That value is populated from
`FileHasher.segmentedHash` (`VideoScanModel+ProbeEngine.swift:865`); files larger than three
segments are sampled, not fully hashed (`FileHasher.swift:183–196`). Different
files can therefore enter the supposedly proven identity family.

`ArchiveAngelFamilyFacts.swift:85–95,152–176` then proposes and stamps the donor's
date, place, and backup answers. `ArchiveAngelFamilyStamp.swift:93–95` preserves
the donor's confidence, including **known**.

The headless probe used byte-different files with equal sampled hashes, then the
production identity walk and date helpers: recipient A inherited donor B's
`1987-06` with confidence `known`. Full SHA-256 distinguished the fixture files.
The duplicate-group-only control correctly excluded its donor, so removing that
one heuristic edge did not fix the sampled-hash edge.

Use proven, appropriately current identity for equal-file facts. A sampled hash
can nominate a candidate, but cannot authorize known-fact or backup-attestation
inheritance. The older Helper's broad family walk predates S4; the new Angel
inheritance now consumes it as proven identity.

## 2. P1 — undirected trim ancestry transfers a segment's date to other events

`ArchiveAngelShowCopies.swift:120–121` follows `derivedFrom` to both parent and
children, transitively, with no derivation-kind or time-range restriction.
`TrimJob.swift:670–673` creates precisely such links with trim offsets.

Reproduced: a parent tape has two trims from different events. The first trim's
known `1987-06-12` becomes an identity-family donor for the second trim, which
inherits that same known date. The identity walk also reaches the whole tape.

Keep derivation and identity distinct. Date/place inheritance needs direction
and scope/range semantics; a dated segment does not date its parent or sibling.
Backup attestations also need their own applicability rules across derivatives.

## 3. P1 — a filename/duration match can substitute unrelated balanced footage

`ArchiveAngel/Prepare/ArchiveAngelJob.swift:774–776` accepts a sibling
`<stem>_balanced` record with no lineage if its duration is within one second.
The code checks existence, but never establishes that its picture/audio derives
from the selected original. It then becomes `balancedRecord`, used as the
access-copy source at lines 733–738 and treated as a companion at promotion.

Trigger: catalogued `tape.mov` and unrelated, equal-duration
`tape_balanced.mov`, with the latter's `derivedFrom` unset. The archive can
receive an access version of the wrong recording under the original's facts.
Require established provenance or explicit confirmation before substitution;
name and duration may only suggest the candidate. Source-confirmed, not executed
through ffmpeg or the app.

## 4. P1 — rollback journal is cleared before restored catalog values are durable

`ArchiveAngelFamilyFacts.restore:243–261` restores values and announces record
changes. The model observer (`VideoScanModel.swift:1219`) schedules catalog
persistence; `CatalogStore.scheduleSave:890–903` waits two seconds before an
asynchronous save. In contrast, `ArchiveAngelPromoter.settleStampedFacts:378`
immediately clears `stampedFacts`, and `settle:310` durably saves the plan.

Concrete failure sequence: inherited values are persisted during a long promote;
the promote is cancelled; the ready plan is saved without its rollback journal;
the process crashes before the delayed catalog save. Restart loads the inherited
values and has no journal left to undo them.

Initial journal persistence is also unchecked: `ArchiveAngelPromoter.swift:215`
ignores `saveLogged`'s Boolean result and starts promotion at line 217.

Require successful durable journal persistence before proceeding, and retain
rollback entries until the restored catalog is durably saved. Source-confirmed
persistence ordering; no process-kill or app integration test was run.

## Corrections that do work

- Duplicate-group-only relatives are shown without lending facts (runtime control).
- Ordinary date/place rollback compares current and written values, preserving
  intervening edits (source review).
- New optional plan fields preserve compatibility with older JSON (source review).
