lizard over the app's Swift code (app, VideoScanCore, swift_cli); test beds and support scripts are not measured. An offender is a function with cyclomatic complexity (CCN) over 15 or more than 80 lines of code. The nightly ratchet reports new offenders and known ones that got worse; the pre-commit hook and CI block anything new or worse over CCN 30 or 300 lines.
